R&D · data-viz experiment

Messi, off the curve

I took 60 years of World Cups and plotted them on a volume × efficiency map to answer, with data, a single question from the terraces.

⚽ World Cups 1966–2026* 📊 source: FBref 🔬 method > shouting
the finding

With the partial data from the 2026 World Cup, Messi became the all-time top scorer in World Cup history (18 goals, passing Klose's 16) and sits alone in a region of the map I named “No Man's Land”: high volume and high efficiency, where nobody else reaches.

01 · The hypothesis

Turning the GOAT debate into something measurable.

The “who's the best” argument is usually loud and hard to falsify. So I turned it into a question with a data answer:

If we look only at World Cups (the biggest stage) and only at forwards, is there anyone who is simultaneously durable, productive and creative at Messi's level?

If the answer is “yes, several”, his profile is nothing special. If it's “no, none”, there's an anomaly worth a chart.

02 · The methodology

One map, three dimensions of information.

AxisMeaningHow it's computed
X — VolumeWorld Cup career minutessum of minutes across all editions
Y — EfficiencyGoals + Assists per 90′(G + A) / minutes × 90
ColorCreationtotal assists (teal scale)

The rules that keep the comparison fair:

  • Forwards only — apples to apples.
  • At least 90′ played (one full match) to enter the map.
  • Messi is drawn as a star and the high-volume + high-efficiency zone is highlighted.
  • Pre-Opta legends (Kocsis 1954, Fontaine 1958, Pelé 1958–70) enter in gold with an asterisk: in their era assists weren't recorded, so their ratio is goals-only, kept apart from the main cloud.

Data from FBref (1966–2022). The in-progress 2026 World Cup deltas were added by hand from press reports (FBref hasn't published them yet) and are flagged as partial. Portraits via Wikipedia/Wikimedia.

03 · The findings

Messi is the only one who lands all three podiums at once.

#1

Durability

2484′ — the only player with +2000′ in World Cups.

#1

Output

24 goal contributions (G+A) in total.

#1

Creation

18 goals + 6 assists: leads both finishing and passing.

Others reach one corner, never all three: Klose and Cristiano have the mileage but a low ratio; Mbappé, Kocsis and Fontaine have an elite ratio but lack volume; Pelé reigns in titles (3) but the modern dataset doesn't rate him by ratio. The result: a statistical anomaly sitting alone in No Man's Land.

⚠️ Honesty first: the “all-time top scorer” headline uses partial 2026 World Cup data. Once FBref publishes the official figures, the numbers update. The experiment is about the method, not the hype.
